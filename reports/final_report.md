# A/B Test Executive Summary: Cookie Cats Gate Move

## Question
Does moving the progression gate from level 30 to level 40 improve player retention without hurting engagement?[cite: 1]

## Result
The absolute difference in retained_d7 between treatment and control was **-0.0068** (p-value: 0.38672).

## Confidence
**High.** The data passed all pipeline validation gates, including a strict Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) check and duplicate user scan prior to statistical analysis. 

## Impact
A shift of -0.68% in 7-day retention applied across the active player base. 

## Recommendation and Next Step
**Flat / Inconclusive**
No statistically meaningful difference. Choose based on cost or simplicity.
